Wealth Wisdom

6 Rules of Smart Money

01

Pay Yourself First

Before any expense hits, automatically transfer at least 20% of every income into savings or investments. You'll never miss what you never see.

02

The 50-30-20 Rule

50% for needs (rent, food, bills), 30% for wants (dining, entertainment), 20% for savings & investments. A simple framework that actually works.

03

Start a SIP Today

A ₹2,000/month SIP started at age 25 can grow to over ₹1 crore by age 55 (at 12% returns). Waiting 5 years cuts that number nearly in half.

04

Kill High-Interest Debt First

Credit card debt at 36% APR is a guaranteed 36% loss on your money. Paying it off is the best "investment" you can make — better than any mutual fund.

05

Build a 3-Month Emergency Fund

Before you invest a single rupee, save 3 months of expenses in a liquid account. This prevents you from selling investments at a loss during tough times.

06

Inflation Is Your Silent Enemy

At 6% inflation, ₹1 lakh today is worth only ₹74,000 in 5 years. Any money not earning above inflation is quietly losing value. Put it to work.
